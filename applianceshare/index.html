Rent kitchen appliances from your neighbors.

ApplianceShare enables neighbors to rent out their idle kitchen appliances by the hour, providing affordable access to specialty tools and generating extra income for owners.

Key Features

Appliance Listing

Owners can upload photos, set hourly rates, describe condition, and specify availability calendar.

Location-Based Search

Renters can search for appliances by type, distance, and availability, with map integration.

Hourly Booking & Calendar

Renters can book specific time slots, and owners manage bookings with a shared calendar.

Integrated Payment & Deposit

Secure payment processing with hourly billing, security deposit hold, and automated release after return.

Liability & Insurance Coverage

Built-in protection plan covering damage, theft, or accidents during rental period, with clear terms for both parties.

How It Works

List or Browse

Owners post their idle appliances with photos and rates. Renters search nearby for what they need.

Book by the Hour

Choose available time slots, pay securely, and a security deposit is held automatically.

Use and Return

Pick up the appliance, use it worry-free with insurance coverage, and return it on time. Payment is released to the owner.
