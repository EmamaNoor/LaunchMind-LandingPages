Clinic management that puts patients first

CliniCore is a cloud-based clinic management system that streamlines appointment scheduling, patient records, billing, and inventory for small to medium-sized clinics, enabling staff to focus on patient care.

Get Started

Everything your clinic needs

Tools designed to reduce administrative burden and improve patient outcomes.

📅

Appointment Scheduling

Online booking, calendar management, automated reminders, and waitlist management to reduce no-shows and optimize doctor time.

📋

Electronic Health Records (EHR)

Secure digital patient charts with visit history, medications, allergies, and lab results, accessible from any device.

💰

Billing & Invoicing

Generate invoices, process payments, track claims, and manage insurance billing to streamline revenue cycle.

📦

Inventory Management

Track medical supplies and medications, set reorder alerts, and manage stock levels to prevent shortages.

📊

Reporting & Analytics

Customizable dashboards and reports on key metrics like patient volume, revenue, and appointment utilization for data-driven decisions.

Get your clinic running on CliniCore in three simple steps.

1

Set Up Your Clinic
