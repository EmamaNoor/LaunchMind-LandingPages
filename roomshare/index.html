Key Features

Room Discovery and Booking

Freelancers can browse nearby buildings, view room photos, amenities, and book instantly with calendar integration.

Real-Time Availability

Live syncing with building management systems to show up-to-the-minute open slots and prevent double bookings.

Pricing Model

Flexible hourly and daily rates set by building managers, with dynamic pricing for peak vs. off-peak hours.

Verification for Building Access

Secure identity verification (ID upload, social login) and QR-code-based entry to ensure only confirmed users enter.

Cancellation Policy

Clear, tiered cancellation rules (free up to 2 hours before, partial refund after) with automated reminders.
