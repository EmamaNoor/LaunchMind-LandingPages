How FixMate works

Simple Job Posting

Homeowners can quickly create a job post with photos, category, and description. No lengthy forms, just the details needed for the repair.

Tradesperson Profile

Retired tradespeople showcase their skills, years of experience, availability, and preferred job types. Set your limits and get matched.

In-App Messaging

Secure, real-time messaging lets homeowners and tradespeople discuss job details, ask questions, and coordinate schedules without sharing phone numbers.

Rating System

Both parties rate each other after job completion. Build trust and accountability with honest feedback from every transaction.

Integrated Payment

Secure in-app payment with escrow ensures tradespeople get paid promptly and homeowners only release funds after satisfactory work.
