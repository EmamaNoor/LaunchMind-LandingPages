Buy and sell course notes with confidence.

StudySwap is a trusted marketplace where university students can buy and sell high-quality handwritten notes and study guides tailored to their specific courses and universities.

Everything you need for smart studying

Note Upload & Preview

Sellers can upload handwritten or digital notes as PDFs with a preview option; buyers can view a few pages before purchasing.

Course & University Filtering

Buyers can search and filter by university, department, course code, and professor to find exactly relevant materials.

Rating & Review System

Buyers can rate notes (1-5 stars) and leave reviews; sellers build reputation; low-rated listings are deprioritized.

Secure Payment Escrow

Payments are held in escrow until the buyer confirms receipt of the full, undamaged notes; supports credit/debit cards and PayPal.

Plagiarism & Quality Check

Automated scan of uploaded notes for plagiarism and basic quality (e.g., legibility, completeness) before listing goes live.

1
