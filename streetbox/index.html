Each month, a curated kit of authentic street food ingredients and recipes from a different Pakistani city. Recreate iconic local flavors at home.

For food enthusiasts, diaspora Pakistanis, and adventurous home cooks.

What's Inside Each Box

Monthly City Theme

Each month highlights a different Pakistani city (e.g., Lahore, Karachi, Peshawar) with a curated selection of its iconic street food ingredients and a signature dish.

Curated Ingredient Kit

Includes 3-4 essential, hard-to-find ingredients (spices, chutneys, pre-mixes) sourced directly from the featured city, plus a bonus snack or condiment.

Recipe Card & Video Tutorial

A beautifully designed recipe card with step-by-step instructions and a QR code linking to a short video tutorial by a local street food vendor or chef.

Optional Spice Level Customization

Subscribers can choose mild, medium, or hot spice level for the kit, ensuring the dish suits their palate.

Community Recipe Swap & Feedback

A private online group where subscribers share their creations, tips, and photos, and vote on future city themes.

How It Works

Choose Your Plan
