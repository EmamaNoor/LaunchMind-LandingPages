StreetEats Get Started

StreetEats delivers a monthly subscription box of curated local street food ingredients from a different Pakistani city, enabling foodies, diaspora Pakistanis, and adventurous home cooks to recreate authentic street food experiences at home.

Perfect for foodies, diaspora Pakistanis, and adventurous home cooks who want to explore authentic street food from Pakistan without the hassle of sourcing hard-to-find ingredients.

Features

City-Themed Box Curation

Each month features a different Pakistani city (e.g., Lahore, Karachi, Peshawar) with a curated selection of its iconic street food ingredients, including authentic spices, sauces, and key staples.

Recipe Cards & Video Tutorials

Includes illustrated step-by-step recipe cards for 3-4 dishes per box, plus QR codes linking to video tutorials by local chefs for an immersive cooking experience.

Subscription Management Portal

Online dashboard where users can manage their subscription (pause, skip, cancel, update address, and view upcoming boxes) with flexible monthly, quarterly, or annual plans.

Personalized Flavor Profile Quiz

Onboarding quiz that captures spice tolerance, dietary restrictions, and flavor preferences to tailor box contents and suggest city themes.

Unboxing AR Experience

Augmented reality feature within the app that, when scanning the box, displays city landmarks, street food history, and chef tips, enhancing the unboxing moment.

How It Works

STEP 01

Take Your Flavor Profile Quiz

Start by completing a quick quiz about your spice tolerance, dietary needs, and flavor preferences. This helps us tailor each box to your tastes.

STEP 02

Receive Your Monthly City Box

Each month, get a curated box of authentic street food ingredients from a featured Pakistani city, complete with recipe cards and video tutorials.

STEP 03

Cook, Explore, and Connect

Follow the recipes, scan the box for an AR city tour, and recreate iconic street food dishes at home. Manage your subscription easily online.
