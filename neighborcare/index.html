Key Features

Neighborhood Matching

Automatically matches hiring parents with nearby stay-at-home parents based on location and availability.

Hourly Booking

Allows hiring parents to book care by the hour with real-time availability and instant confirmation.

Background Check Integration

Integrates with third-party background check services to verify all providers before they can accept bookings.

In-App Messaging

Enables secure, in-app communication between hiring parents and providers to discuss details and confirm arrangements.

Payment Processing

Handles secure payments per booking, including automatic payouts to providers and receipts for hiring parents.

Ratings & Reviews

Allows both parties to rate and review each other after each booking to build trust and accountability.

How It Works

1

Sign up and set your needs

Busy parents quickly register with their address and child's age. Stay-at-home parents set their after-school availability.

2

Match and book

Search for providers in your neighborhood, view ratings, background check status, and hourly rates. Book with instant confirmation.

3

Care and get paid

After each booking, providers receive prompt payments to their bank account. Both parties leave reviews to maintain quality standards.
