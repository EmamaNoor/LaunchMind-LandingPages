Fresh pastries at half price. No waste.

BakeSave connects small bakeries with budget-conscious consumers and food waste warriors to sell end-of-day surplus items at a discount, reducing waste and saving money.

Bakery Onboarding

Simple sign-up for bakeries with verification of business details, location, and operating hours.

Daily Surplus Listing

Bakeries can quickly list leftover items with photos, descriptions, quantities, and discount prices each day.

Real-Time Inventory

Automatically updates available items as users claim and purchase, preventing overselling.

Geolocation Search

Users can browse nearby bakeries with surplus deals on an interactive map or list sorted by distance.

Pickup Ordering

Users can claim and pay for items in-app, receiving a QR code for pickup during specified time windows.

How it works

1

Bakeries list surplus

At the end of each day, bakeries post leftover items with a discount price and pickup window.

2

Find deals near you

Browse nearby bakeries on the map, see what's available, and claim items in seconds.

3

Pick up and enjoy

Pay in the app, get a QR code, and collect your order at the bakery during the pickup time.
