Everything you need to control cloud spend

Six capabilities that cover detection, optimization, commitment management, and reporting across your AWS accounts.

01

Cost Anomaly Detection

Automatically detects unusual spending patterns using machine learning and sends alerts via Slack, email, or PagerDuty.

02

Rightsizing Recommendations

Analyzes usage metrics to recommend optimal instance types and sizes, with projected savings and one-click apply.

03

Reserved Instance (RI) Management

Tracks RI utilization and coverage, recommends purchases, and automates RI exchanges and modifications.

04

Automated Savings Actions

Executes predefined actions like stopping idle instances, deleting unattached volumes, and scheduling start/stop times.

05

Cost Allocation and Tagging

Enforces tagging policies, provides cost breakdown by team, project, or environment, and enables chargeback/showback.

06

Savings Dashboard and Reporting

Visualizes cost trends, savings achieved, and forecasted spend with customizable reports for stakeholders.

Connect your AWS accounts

Apply savings actions
