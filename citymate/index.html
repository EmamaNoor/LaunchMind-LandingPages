CityMate connects newly arrived expats with verified local helpers to handle essential setup tasks in a new city, paid per task.

From housing tours to bank setup. Skip the stress, get real help.

Everything you need to settle in

Expat Onboarding

Guided profile setup capturing arrival date, languages, and immediate needs; includes identity verification and a welcome checklist.

Task Browsing & Booking

Expats can browse helpers by task categories (e.g., housing tours, bank setup, SIM card) and location, view profiles, and book a task with fixed fee or hourly rate.

Helper Registration & Availability

Local helpers sign up with verification, set task categories they offer (e.g., airport pickup, document translation), define their hourly rate or fixed fee, and mark availability on a calendar.

Task Management & Confirmation

Helpers receive task requests with details, can accept/decline, and both parties get real-time updates; includes in-app chat for coordination.

Secure Payment & Escrow

Payment is held in escrow until task completion; expat confirms completion, then funds are released to helper minus platform fee. Supports multiple currencies.

How it works

1

Create your profile

Sign up as an expat or a local helper. Complete verification and set your preferences, languages, and availability.

2

Browse or list tasks

Expats search for helpers by task type and location. Helpers list their services with fixed fees or hourly rates.

3

Book, complete, get paid

Book a helper, communicate via chat, and confirm completion. Payment is released securely from escrow.
